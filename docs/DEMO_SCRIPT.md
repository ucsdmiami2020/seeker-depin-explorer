# Demo video script — Seeker DePIN Explorer

Target: **2:00–2:30**, portrait, screen recording from a Seeker (or Android emulator) with Phantom installed.
Record at 1080×2340. Narrate live or add voiceover afterwards; keep captions on for judges who watch muted.

Before recording: connect a wallet that actually holds at least one DePIN token (HNT, MOBILE, HONEY, GEOD…), so the
holdings section is not empty. Put the phone in Do Not Disturb; hide notifications.

---

### 0:00–0:15 — The problem

> "DePIN is the part of crypto you can actually hold. But if you want to know which hardware is real, what it earns,
> and whether you already own a piece of it, you are stuck with a dozen browser tabs."

Shot: welcome screen, then Enter Explorer.

### 0:15–0:40 — The catalog

> "Seeker DePIN Explorer is a field guide to 17 devices across 14 Solana networks — phones, rings, hotspots,
> dashcams, weather stations."

Shot: scroll Explore. Tap a category chip. Tap the adoption sort.

> "Every device carries an adoption-confidence score built from five evidence factors, so you can tell a shipping
> network from a whitepaper."

Shot: tap a badge → methodology screen → back.

### 0:40–1:05 — Device depth

Shot: open Helium Mobile Hotspot → scroll through specs, "How you earn", timeline.

> "Specs, list price, how the device earns, and the network's history — with every claim disclaimed where rewards are
> involved. Vendor links open in a Chrome Custom Tab, from a fixed allow-list."

Shot: tap a vendor link, show the Custom Tab, come back.

### 1:05–1:45 — The mobile-native part: wallet

> "Here is what makes this a phone app rather than a website."

Shot: Wallet tab → Connect wallet → Phantom approval sheet → back in the app.

> "Mobile Wallet Adapter — the wallet approves on-device. The app never sees a private key, and it cannot build or
> send a transaction."

Shot: SOL balance and the DePIN holdings list.

> "It reads your balances straight from Solana and matches them against the catalog: these are the networks you are
> already part of. Mints are verified, so nothing is mislabelled — and networks we could not verify are called out
> instead of quietly shown as zero."

Shot: tap a holding → jumps to that device → point at the wallet badge on the detail screen.

### 1:45–2:05 — Proof of ownership

Shot: Wallet tab → Sign ownership proof → wallet shows the statement → signature appears.

> "And you can prove you control the address with an off-chain signature — no fee, no transaction. A step towards
> operator-verified reviews, where only people who actually run a Helium hotspot can rate one."

### 2:05–2:25 — Close

Shot: Devnet toggle, then About → Legal.

> "Devnet toggle for reviewers, no accounts, no analytics, no data collection — and the dApp Store paperwork is
> already done. Seeker DePIN Explorer: the physical side of Solana, in your pocket."

---

## Shot checklist

- [ ] Welcome → Enter Explorer
- [ ] Explore: scroll, category chip, adoption sort, methodology screen
- [ ] Device detail: specs, earn, timeline, vendor link in Custom Tab
- [ ] Compare tab: add two devices side by side (optional if time is tight)
- [ ] Wallet: connect via MWA, balance, holdings, tap through to device
- [ ] Device detail with wallet badge visible
- [ ] Sign ownership proof, wallet approval sheet, signature
- [ ] Devnet/Mainnet toggle
- [ ] About → Legal screens

## Do not

- Do not show the seed phrase, recovery flow, or a wallet's private screens.
- Do not show an address you care about keeping private — the recording is public.
- Do not claim rewards, prices or yields that are not on screen.
